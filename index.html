Boot sequence / a short story about how I work

01 / Power on

It starts in the dark.

No audience. No applause. Just a board, a datasheet and the decision to begin.

02 / Clock

Show up on every tick.

A clock does not wait for motivation. Discipline is a signal that never skips a cycle.

03 / Route

Build quietly, trace by trace.

Most of the work is invisible: the routing, the debugging, the nights nobody sees.

04 / Compute

Hustle until the logic holds.

Test. Fail. Rewire. Repeat, until the output stops being luck and becomes design.

05 / Output

Then let the results speak.

I'm Pham Huynh Gia Huy, a Computer Engineering student at HCMUT, building intelligence from the signal up.

02 / About

A student of how machines learn to listen.

Hi, I'm Gia Huy — a first-year Computer Engineering student at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), VNU-HCM.

I have built AI-enabled web applications and retrieval-augmented assistants, most recently CompeTech, a student development platform. I am interested in embedded systems and efficient machine learning at the edge, and I am currently developing the hardware and digital-systems foundation for that work.

Based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam · 10.77° N, 106.66° E

Research interests

  • Embedded systems
  • Efficient ML at the edge
  • Retrieval-augmented generation
  • AI in education

04 / Projects

Selected projects.

i.

CompeTech

AI-enabled student development platform

A web application for tracking student development, reviewing evidence and supporting career exploration, with retrieval-augmented question answering and generative-AI features for CV feedback and career guidance. Built with teacher Nguyen Minh Ly and Nguyen Khanh Nam, it covers conduct scores, activities, career guidance and entrepreneurship, and was piloted at Nguyen Chi Thanh High School. AI suggestions are always reviewed by a teacher.

  • React
  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • Firebase
  • Docker
  • RAG

ii.

How To Love Viet Nam

Product suite for teaching and learning History

A web product suite that supports the teaching and learning of History and local educational content in Ho Chi Minh City, with generative-AI assistance, flashcards, e-learning lectures and mind maps. First Prize at HSU Programming Challenge 2025, Second Prize at the HCMC Science and Engineering Fair and at the 6th HUIT Startup competition, and a Silver Medal at the HCMC Design, Fabrication and Application Awards.
